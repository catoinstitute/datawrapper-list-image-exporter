Cato.org article chart resizer

Pulls a published Datawrapper chart, lets you crop or trim it, and exports a 1920x1080 PNG centered in a true 16:9 frame.

1. Load your chart
Paste a Datawrapper chart link or just its ID. This pulls in a previously published PNG export.

Set up a one-time button in your bookmarks bar that lists every Datawrapper chart on a cato.org page.

  1. Show your bookmarks bar: Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+B.
  2. Drag this onto it: Find charts
  3. Click it on any cato.org page with Datawrapper charts, or try it on this article.
2. Adjust
Trim the edges or cut a gap out of the middle. Crop top/bottom lets you drag either edge inward to trim it off. Remove gap lets you cut a band out of the middle, like empty space above the sources line, and join what's left. Show plain version swaps in Datawrapper's export without the title, notes and sources, which starts you over from a fresh image. What you see here is exactly what gets exported.
