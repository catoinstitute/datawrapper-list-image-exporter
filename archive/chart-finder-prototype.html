Cato.org article chart resizer

Pulls a published Datawrapper chart, lets you crop or trim it, and exports a 1920x1080 PNG centered in a true 16:9 frame. Ready to drop into a blog post or article.

1. Load your chart
Paste a Datawrapper chart link or just its ID. This pulls the published PNG export directly from Datawrapper's CDN.

Paste the cato.org page the chart appears on. Click the chart you want and it loads straight into the tool.

2. Adjust
Trim the edges or cut a gap out of the middle. Crop top/bottom lets you drag either edge inward to trim it off. Remove gap lets you cut a band out of the middle, like empty space above the sources line, and join what's left. What you see here is exactly what gets exported.
